## What Is JMRI DecoderPro

JMRI DecoderPro is a local Windows decoder helper for locomotives you already own. It gives you a named decoder desk, a short roster list, and a cv view that points back to the same loco. It does not try to own every window on the PC. It sits beside the roster you already keep and holds only the decoder that is open now.
The idea behind JMRI DecoderPro own locos is small on purpose: one desk, one list, one path back. Extra CVs wait for their own title. JMRI DecoderPro starts with a local profile and does not need a complex account system.
People who keep a home roster, a club locomotive they administer, or a decoder they already own use JMRI DecoderPro decoder desk in different ways, but the job stays the same. Untitled copies leave the live set. The last handoff stays on the loco you keep.
